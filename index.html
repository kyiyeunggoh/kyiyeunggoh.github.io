How I work

Broad enough to connect the system. Technical enough to get into the details.

01

Start with the behaviour, not the feature. Understand how people, institutions and adversaries actually behave before choosing the intervention.

02

Build to learn. I prefer a working prototype and real user feedback over extended debate about a hypothetical product.

03

Design the operating system, not just the interface. At scale, ownership, policy, incentives, data flows and operational workflows matter as much as UX.

04

Stay close to implementation. I’m comfortable moving between strategy, product specs, data, model behaviour, process design and stakeholder alignment.

About

I’m interested in how technology can be used to make people’s lives better.

That has taken me through policy, civic tech, public-sector transformation, AI safety and education. The common thread is less about a particular industry than a type of problem: systems where technology directly shapes what people can do, what they experience, and whether institutions work as intended.

Outside work, I run DatastorySG and keep building small products. I’m particularly interested in roles at the intersection of AI, trust & safety, education and public-interest technology.
